Holiday Light Spectacular

Logo files for the Holiday Light Spectacular, ready to use on the web, in documents, and in QR codes.

Purpose

Logo files for the Holiday Light Spectacular, ready to use on the web, in documents, and in QR codes.

  • Use the supplied logo files so the Holiday Light Spectacular looks the same everywhere.
  • Give staff and partners a white and a transparent version to choose from.

Logos

  • Use the supplied artwork. Do not recolor, stretch, or redraw the logo.
  • Leave clear space around the logo and do not overlap it with other graphics.

Logo, white background Preferred

Round yellow logo with red Holiday Light Spectacular lettering and a string of colored lights, on a white background.

Use in documents, slides, and web pages with a white or light background.

Logo, transparent background

The same logo with nothing behind the circle.

Use on colored pages or over other artwork, where a white box around the logo would not fit.

QR code logos

The default is the round logo on a transparent square. Use it for the center of a QR code.

QR code logo

Round Holiday Light Spectacular logo on a transparent square.

Use as the logo in the center of a QR code for the Holiday Light Spectacular.

Color palette

Yellow and red are the main colors in the logo.

Green, purple, orange, and charcoal appear in the string of lights.

These colors were taken from the logo artwork, not from a style guide. Use the supplied logo files rather than re-creating them from these values.
